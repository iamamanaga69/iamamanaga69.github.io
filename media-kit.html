Brand Summary

Flexist Crypto is a premium Web3 growth ecosystem focused on helping crypto projects expand across India. Founded by Aman Agarwal, the platform combines community architecture, India market-entry strategy, creator campaigns, ambassador operations, partnerships, and operator-led growth consulting.

The operating foundation was built across live projects including Fabwelt, RRG Ventures, Unielon Wallet, UXUY Wallet, and Maestro Bots.
