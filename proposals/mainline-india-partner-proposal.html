01

Understanding the objective

The goal is to build Mainline in India around genuine, long-term traders — not registration volume.

That needs four things working together: clean onboarding for Indian users, community support that is visibly active, tracking both sides can trust, and creators whose audiences actually trade. Your model already points the same way — a fixed creator fee scaled to reach and audience quality, $200 per Qualified First-Time Depositor, revenue share for creators who keep performing, and no payout on fake registrations or bot traffic. Everything below is built to produce depositors who trade, because that is the only outcome your model pays for.

02

Scope of work — India Partner

Ten workstreams, all running concurrently for the term.

  1. India market auditWhere Mainline's realistic Indian trader audience sits today — which Telegram groups, YouTube channels, X circles and regional communities discuss the platforms you compete with, and which of them convert.
  2. Community setup and managementTelegram built and staffed (Discord too, if you want it): moderation, response flow, rules, activation calendar. IST coverage, so questions get answered while Indian users are awake.
  3. Unlimited KOL campaignsNo cap on creator count or content cycles for the term. Selection weighted toward trading audiences — comment sections with real questions, not reply farms.
  4. Full ambassador programRecruitment, onboarding, training, task management, contribution scoring and rewards. Ambassadors carry local content, in-community support and regional-language reach.
  5. Monthly partnership pipelineOngoing outreach to Indian trading communities, Web3 projects and media — collab campaigns, community swaps, co-marketing.
  6. Monthly AMA coordinationOne AMA a month with the Indian community: host, promotion, question curation, live moderation and follow-up.
  7. App review managementStructured Play Store / App Store review campaign with real Indian users, plus review monitoring and responses. Drop this line if Mainline is web-only.
  8. Dedicated Indian supportA named point of contact, IST availability, priority response on anything user-facing or time-sensitive.
  9. Full network accessIntroductions across the Indian Web3 network — exchange contacts, trading-community founders, project founders and crypto media.
  10. Weekly founder call30–45 minutes each week: what shipped, what the numbers say, what changes next.

Expectation worth setting

Creator response rates in India run roughly 40–60% even with existing relationships — schedules, exclusivity with competing platforms and internal approvals all get in the way. Expect the first wave to land 5–8 confirmed creators out of 12–15 approached. Better said now than as a shortlist that quietly shrinks over three weeks.

The retainer covers management and execution of everything in Section 02. It does not include money that goes to creators. The creator budget is separate, set per creator, and follows the model you outlined: a fixed fee scaled to reach, engagement and audience quality, plus $200 per QFTD, plus revenue share for top performers.

Approval gate

No creator is committed and no campaign budget is spent without written approval from Mainline first. You receive the shortlist with audience metrics and quoted rates, and approve or decline each name. Your fixed exposure is the retainer; the performance money only moves when genuine depositors do.

On fraud: creators are screened for engagement authenticity before reaching your shortlist, and we do not use incentivised-signup farms or bounty platforms. If a creator's traffic does not convert into real trading activity, we stop the spend and tell you — including when it was a creator we recommended.
