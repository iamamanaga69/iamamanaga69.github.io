Origin Story

Built in the field, not in a slide deck.

Aman Agarwal entered crypto in 2020, when communities carried more signal than most marketing dashboards. The early work happened where Web3 trust was actually formed: Telegram groups, Discord servers, allocation conversations, campaign partnerships, and support threads.

Across Fabwelt, RRG Ventures, Unielon Wallet, UXUY Wallet, and Maestro Bots, one pattern became obvious: projects entering India often treated the market as a traffic source. India needs a different operating model. It needs culture-aware community systems, regional distribution, and execution that can turn curiosity into long-term participation.

Flexist Crypto was built as that operating model. It is not a personal services page. It is a growth ecosystem designed for founders who want durable India expansion.
