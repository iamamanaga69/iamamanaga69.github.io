Origin Story

Built in real crypto communities.

The FLEXIST founder entered crypto in 2020 and learned growth where trust is actually built: Telegram groups, Discord servers, support chats, collab talks, and launch campaigns.

FLEXIST was built to become that India growth partner for Web3 founders.
