Discovery Habits

How Indian crypto users find projects.

Most users first hear about a project from Telegram, YouTube, X, or a trusted friend. After that, they check if the community is active, if admins reply, and if real people are talking about the product.

The winning path is simple: get attention, build trust, answer fast, and give users a reason to stay.

Ambassador Opportunity

Local advocates turn reach into trust.

Indian ambassadors can close the distance between a global product and a local user. They explain, support, collect feedback, surface opportunities, and activate regional networks with context that generic global campaigns cannot provide.

The program only works when contribution is structured: clear tasks, education, accountability, feedback, and recognition.
