Operator Story

Community is where the real signal starts.

I got into crypto in 2020 when most people were still figuring out what Bitcoin was. I didn't come from a hedge fund or a startup accelerator. I came from the community — from Discord servers and Telegram groups, from actually talking to users, understanding their frustrations, building trust at the ground level.

That changed the way I think about growth. Numbers matter, but they only become valuable when people understand why they should stay. Across wallets, communities, collaborations, and ambassador operations, my focus has stayed consistent: turn attention into participation and participation into long-term momentum.
